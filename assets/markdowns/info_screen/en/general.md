This app contains information and tips for heart patients who are about to decide on cardiac rehab or are already on the way there. The content presented is based on the information needs collected from heart patients who took part in the HERO project at the Ludwig Boltzmann Institute for Digital Health & Prevention in Salzburg.

- The sources of information for the contents of the app were:
  - [Gesundheitsportal Österreich](https://www.gesundheit.gv.at/krankheiten/herz-kreislauf/herzinfarkt/rehabilitation.html)
  - [Heart Foundation](https://www.heartfoundation.org.au/getmedia/4d6c4be1-622d-4b49-8ca3-0a5214f03efc/210721-MHML-Book-1-Reboot_SA.pdf)
  - [Rehakompass](https://rehakompass.goeg.at/#/einrichtung/list)
  - Scientific publications
- The tips were formulated by heart patients and health experts as part of the HERO project.
